Safety is our non-negotiable priority. All flights are continuously monitored by our FAA-certified dispatch meteorologists. In the event of low cloud ceiling, heavy rain, or adverse wind conditions, you are immediately eligible for an instant 100% full refund or a complimentary priority reschedule to any date within 24 months — with zero rebooking fees.
Under FAA Part 135 federal regulations, helicopters require meticulous weight and balance calculations prior to every takeoff. Passenger weights are discreetly verified at check-in.
