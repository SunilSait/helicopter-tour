FAQ

Common Questions

What should I wear for a helicopter tour?
We recommend comfortable, casual clothing. Avoid loose items like scarves or hats that could be affected by rotor wash. Closed-toe shoes are required. For high-altitude or cold-weather tours, we provide insulated flight suits.
Can I bring my camera on board?
Absolutely! Cameras, smartphones, and GoPros are welcome. We recommend securing them with a wrist strap or neck strap. Our pilots can position the aircraft to maximize your photo opportunities. We also offer professional photo/video packages.
What happens if the weather is bad?
Passenger safety is our top priority. If weather conditions don't meet our standards, we will reschedule your flight at no charge or provide a full refund. Our team monitors weather continuously and will contact you at least 2 hours before departure.
