Safety Protocols

Our Commitment to Safety

Every flight operates under our multi-layered safety management system — because there are no second chances in aviation.

Daily Pre-Flight Inspections

Every aircraft undergoes a comprehensive 47-point mechanical inspection before its first flight of the day. Our licensed A&P mechanics certify each aircraft as airworthy every morning.

Advanced Weather Monitoring

We use commercial-grade meteorological systems and maintain direct communication with local ATC. Flights are cancelled or modified at the first sign of suboptimal conditions.

Recurrent Pilot Training

All pilots undergo bi-annual simulator training, annual medical examinations, and regular proficiency checks. We exceed FAA minimum training requirements by 40%.

Emergency Preparedness

Every aircraft is equipped with an emergency locator transmitter (ELT), first aid kit, and fire suppression system. All ground crew are emergency medical trained.

Weight & Balance Calculations

Precise weight and balance calculations are performed for every flight. Passenger seating is scientifically arranged to ensure optimal aircraft performance and stability.

GPS Flight Tracking

All flights are tracked in real-time via ADS-B and GPS systems. Our operations center monitors every aircraft continuously from departure to landing.
