Our Story

Born from a Passion for the Sky

Founded in 2008 by veteran pilot Captain Michael Torres, SkyVista began with a single helicopter and an unwavering belief: everyone deserves to see the world from above.

Today, we operate a fleet of 12 modern aircraft, employing 24 FAA-certified pilots across 6 scenic locations. Our commitment to safety, luxury, and unforgettable memories remains unchanged.
