Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To deliver safe, inspiring, and deeply educational aerial journeys that grant travelers rare, unforgettable perspectives on our planet's greatest natural and cultural marvels — elevated by five-star hospitality.

Zero-Compromise Safety: Operating far above statutory FAA requirements with daily multi-point inspection protocols.

Quiet-Technology Leadership: Investing exclusively in Fenestron shrouded-rotor helicopters (50% quieter than standard models).

Our Journey

16 Years of Elevation: Milestone by Milestone

From a single helicopter on a desert apron to a world-class fleet operating across three continents.

2010

The First Ascent

Captain Marcus Vance launches SkyVista with 1 Bell 206 JetRanger in Las Vegas, offering sunset flights into the Grand Canyon West Rim with live pilot narration.

2013

FAA Part 135 Commercial Certification

Achieved stringent FAA Part 135 on-demand air carrier status (#SV-94821), implementing airline-grade safety management systems (SMS) and strict maintenance logs.

2016

Island Expansion: Hawaii Bases

Opened full bases in Kauai and Maui, pioneering eco-conscious coastal routes over Na Pali's 4,000-ft sea cliffs and the remote interior waterfalls of Mount Waialeale.

2019

The ECO-Star Revolution

Transitioned primary sightseeing fleet to Airbus H130 ECO-Star helicopters featuring 180° curved wraparound glass, theater-style seating, and whisper-quiet Fenestron technology.

2022

FAA Diamond Award of Excellence

Honored with the FAA Diamond Award for 100% technician certification and surpassing 10,000 continuous flight hours with zero reportable incidents or deviations.

Today & Beyond

Global Reach, Sustainable Horizons

Operating 48 scenic routes from 6 premier global hubs with over 12,500 delighted passengers, while pioneering carbon-neutral flight initiatives and eVTOL research.

Guiding Philosophy
